PoplessRebel's Silksong Any% Gauntlet 2026

The PoplessRebel's Silksong Any% Gauntlet 2026 is a knockout-style speedrunning tournament for the Silksong Any% NMG category. The tournament took place on August 15th and 16th, 2026.

Runs could be done on either route, but runs doing Key Route would be given a +1:40 time penalty due to it being inherently faster than Bell Route.

There was a limit of 10 entrants for the tournament, and over the course of four rounds the slowest runners would be knocked out until only one winner remained. There was a $100 prize pool for this tournament.
