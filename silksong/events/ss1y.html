Silksong 1st Anniversary Celebration

SS1Y is the Silksong first anniversary event! SS1Y will include main board, category extension, and bingo showcases, and will be spread out over the course of one weekend. Bingy Day will take place on September 5th, 2026 and the main event will take place on September 6th, 2026.
