All Majors Randomizer Tournament December 2026

For more information on randomizer tournaments like this one, see the randomizer tournaments page.

This tournament is a one-day tournament taking place on December 5th, 2026. The tournament will consist of three rounds: a qualifier, semi-final, and final round. Each round consists of all of the remaining teams playing the same seed concurrently, with the top finishing teams moving onto the next round.

All Majors is a semi-spoiler format. Each team has a counter of major items remaining in each area, but they don't know what the items are or their exact locations. Their goal is to find all of these major items and finish with both players defeating the Radiance! In this tournament, fragile charms will not be majors and the items at unbreakable charm locations will be vanilla.

Gimmicks

"Gimmicks" are extra settings added to the base tourney settings to spice up the match. This tournament will be played without gimmicks for the first two rounds, while the final round can add up to two gimmicks. To select gimmicks, both teams are required to offer the other a list of three gimmicks, and one will be selected from the list by the opposing team. A list of gimmicks for this tournament can be found below:

Ghosts + Dream trees

The Real Journal Rando (entries only, no cursed locations)

“Advanced” skips turned on

Grubs randomized

Random Bench Location (Minimum 2 benches per area)

Nail upgrades (+ Pale ore dupes)

Map Area Rando
