Disney's All Routes Any% Tournament 2026

This tournament is a double-elimination tournament for the Any% category. Unlike previous tournaments, runners can run any route and slower routes can still be competitive! Instead of determining the winner of a match by giving the win to the person with a faster time, the relative speed of the routes chosen is taken into account as well. This means that a runner can in theory win a race with a slower time if the route they were using is inherently slower. Matches are restreamed on the HollowKnightCommunity Twitch channel. The tournament began on August 13th 2026 and is currently ongoing.

The comparison between final times is done as follows: if one runner is playing on patch 1.2.2.1 and the other is on 1.4.3.2+, the 1.4.3.2+ runner will have time subtracted from their final time to account for the route difference. 60s will be subtracted for Isma's route and 90s will be subtracted for Cdash route. If both runners are playing on the same patch, no time will be subtracted from either time before comparing the final times.
